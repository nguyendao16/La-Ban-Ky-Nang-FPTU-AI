CLO–JD refactor on 2026-10-17. Original source bytes are recorded in manifest.json.

here. These files are outside the runtime and default pytest discovery.

supported entrypoints. Frozen research manifests retain their original paths and
fingerprints; do not edit them to execute a run against the new implementation.
